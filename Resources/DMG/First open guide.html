Opening Joe the first time

Joe isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. After that it opens normally.

  1. Drag Joe onto Applications in the installer window, then eject the installer.
  2. Open Applications and double-click Joe.
  3. If macOS says "Joe" can't be opened or Apple could not verify "Joe", click Done (not Move to Trash).
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Joe and click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password.
  5. Click Open once more. Joe starts its setup.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) you can instead right-click Joe in Applications and choose Open.

During setup Joe asks for

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: so you can talk to Joe.
  • Accessibility: so Joe's shortcut key works in every app. macOS opens Settings; switch Joe on.
  • Automation: so Joe can control apps like Music or Calendar when you ask.

Click Allow each time. If you skipped one, Joe's setup lets you fix it later.
