# Language B: Chinese (Mandarin) (SL/HL)
Group 2 (Language acquisition) · SL 150 h, HL 240 h
Aliases: Chinese B, Mandarin B, Chinese B SL, Chinese B HL, 中文B

## How it's assessed (SL and HL share the outline)
| Component | Weight | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 · Productive skills: one writing task from a choice of three, in a stated text type | 25% | SL about 300–480 characters, HL about 450–720 (check with the teacher); register and text-type conventions are assessed |
| Paper 2 · Receptive skills: listening (three audio texts) and reading (three written texts) | 50% (25% + 25%) | Comprehension questions in Chinese |
| Individual oral (IA): SL a visual stimulus linked to a theme; HL a literary extract; then conversation | 25% | 12–15 minutes with preparation time |
No prescribed topics: five themes with recommended topics.

## Five themes and recommended topics
- Identities 身份认同: lifestyles, health and well-being, beliefs and values, subcultures, language and identity
- Experiences 经历: leisure, holidays and travel, life stories, rites of passage, customs and traditions, migration
- Human ingenuity 人类的创造力: entertainment, artistic expressions, communication and media, technology, scientific innovation
- Social organization 社会组织: social relationships, community, social engagement, education, the working world, law and order
- Sharing the planet 共享地球: the environment, human rights, peace and conflict, equality, globalization, ethics, urban and rural environment

## Text types for Paper 1 and their conventions
- Personal: 日记 diary (date, first person, reflective), 博客 blog (title, date, engaging opening, invites comments), 个人信件/电子邮件 personal letter or email (称呼, greeting, closing 祝你…, signature, date)
- Professional: 正式信件 formal letter (respectful 称呼 尊敬的…, 此致 敬礼), 报告 report (title, headings, objective tone), 提案 proposal, 简历 CV, 问卷 questionnaire
- Mass media: 文章 article (title, by-line, paragraphs), 演讲稿 speech (各位老师、同学们, rhetorical questions, 谢谢大家), 宣传册 brochure/leaflet (headings, persuasive), 采访 interview (Q&A layout), 评论 review, 新闻报道 news report, 社交媒体帖子 social media post

## Grammar and structures worth drilling
了 (completed action and change), 过 (experience), 着 (continuous state), 在/正在 (progressive), 把 and 被 sentences, result and directional complements (看完, 听懂, 走进来), potential complements (看得懂/看不懂), 是…的 emphasis, comparisons (比, 没有…那么, 跟…一样), 越来越, 越…越…, 虽然…但是, 不但…而且, 因为…所以, 如果…就, 只要…就, 只有…才, 除了…以外, 对…来说, 一边…一边, 既…又, 不仅…还, 连…都/也, 难道…吗, measure words.

## How to quiz it
- Vocabulary by theme: give the English or a Chinese definition, ask for the word (and pinyin); or the word, ask for a sentence using it. Aim around HSK 4–5.
- Grammar: ask them to correct a sentence, or translate a short sentence using a target pattern.
- Text types: give a Paper 1 style prompt (e.g. 你的学校组织了一次环保活动。写一篇博客…) and ask what the opening and closing should look like, or ask for a short paragraph and correct it.
- Reading/listening style: give a short passage in simplified Chinese and ask comprehension questions in Chinese.
- Oral practice: describe a visual stimulus in words (a photo of a crowded city, people recycling) and ask them to speak about it as in the oral, then ask follow-up questions.
- Feedback: correct errors explicitly (原句 → 改正), keep explanations short, in English unless they ask for Chinese.
