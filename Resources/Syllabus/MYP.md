# IB Middle Years Programme (MYP)
Ages 11–16, years 1–5. Every subject is assessed against four criteria, A to D, each out of 8.
Aliases: MYP, Middle Years

## Criteria by subject group (each 0–8)
| Subject group | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Language and literature | Analysing | Organizing | Producing text | Using language |
| Language acquisition | Listening | Reading | Speaking | Writing |
| Individuals and societies | Knowing and understanding | Investigating | Communicating | Thinking critically |
| Sciences | Knowing and understanding | Inquiring and designing | Processing and evaluating | Reflecting on the impacts of science |
| Mathematics | Knowing and understanding | Investigating patterns | Communicating | Applying mathematics in real-life contexts |
| Arts | Investigating | Developing | Creating/performing | Evaluating |
| Physical and health education | Knowing and understanding | Planning for performance | Applying and performing | Reflecting and improving performance |
| Design | Inquiring and analysing | Developing ideas | Creating the solution | Evaluating |

## Levels
Each criterion has four bands: 1–2 (limited), 3–4 (adequate), 5–6 (substantial), 7–8 (excellent). 0 = nothing that reaches level 1.
The best-fit band is chosen from the descriptor, then the higher or lower mark within it.

## Final grade (1–7) from the total of the four criteria (out of 32)
1–5 → 1 · 6–9 → 2 · 10–14 → 3 · 15–18 → 4 · 19–23 → 5 · 24–27 → 6 · 28–32 → 7

## Framework
- Global contexts: identities and relationships; orientation in space and time; personal and cultural expression; scientific and technical innovation; globalization and sustainability; fairness and development.
- Key concepts (16): aesthetics, change, communication, communities, connections, creativity, culture, development, form, global interactions, identity, logic, perspective, relationships, systems, time place and space.
- ATL skills: communication, social, self-management, research, thinking.
- Personal Project (MYP 5): criterion A Planning, B Applying skills, C Reflecting (each 0–8); a product plus a report.
- MYP 5 eAssessment: on-screen exams in language and literature, individuals and societies, sciences, mathematics (standard or extended), and an interdisciplinary exam; ePortfolios for language acquisition, arts, PHE, design.

## How to quiz MYP students
- Say which criterion a question targets ("Criterion A: Knowing and understanding").
- Mark with the band, the mark out of 8 and why, using the descriptor's language: "states" (1–2), "outlines" (3–4), "describes" (5–6), "explains" (7–8) for knowing and understanding.
- Questions fit the year: MYP 1–3 simpler contexts, MYP 4–5 closer to eAssessment style, with real-world contexts and a global context named.
