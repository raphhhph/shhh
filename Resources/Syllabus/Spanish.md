# Spanish: Language B (SL/HL) and ab initio (SL)
Group 2 (Language acquisition) · Language B first assessment 2020; ab initio first assessment 2020 · 150 h SL, 240 h HL
Aliases: Spanish B, Spanish ab initio, Spanish, Español, Spanish B SL, Spanish B HL

## How it's assessed
| Component | Spanish ab initio (SL) | Spanish B SL | Spanish B HL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 · Writing: Language B one task from a choice of three; ab initio two tasks, each from a choice of three | 1h, two tasks of 70–150 words, 25% | 1h15, 250–400 words, 25% | 1h30, 450–600 words, 25% |
| Paper 2 · Listening and reading (three texts each) | 45 min + 1h, 50% | 45 min + 1h, 50% | 1h + 1h, 50% |
| Individual oral (IA) | visual stimulus on a prescribed topic, 7–10 min, 25% | visual stimulus linked to a theme, 12–15 min, 25% | extract from a literary work studied, 12–15 min, 25% |
Writing is marked on language, message and conceptual understanding (text type, register, audience, purpose). Ab initio also requires knowledge of the culture of Spanish-speaking countries.

## Themes
- Language B: identities, experiences, human ingenuity, social organization, sharing the planet (same recommended topics as every Language B: lifestyles, health, beliefs, migration, customs, media, technology, education, work, environment, human rights, globalization…).
- Ab initio: the same five themes with 20 prescribed topics: personal attributes, personal relationships, eating and drinking, physical well-being; daily routine, leisure, holidays, festivals and celebrations; transport, entertainment, media, technology; neighbourhood, education, the workplace, social issues; climate, physical geography, the environment, global issues.

## Text types for Paper 1
- Personal: diario, blog, correo electrónico or carta informal (Querido/a…, Un abrazo, Besos)
- Professional: carta formal (Estimado/a señor/a…, Le saluda atentamente), informe, propuesta, correo formal, instrucciones
- Mass media: artículo (título, subtítulos), discurso (Buenos días a todos…, preguntas retóricas, Muchas gracias por su atención), folleto, entrevista, reseña, noticia, publicación en redes sociales
Ab initio adds simpler types: postal, nota, mensaje, lista, invitación, anuncio.

## Grammar worth drilling
- Ab initio core: ser vs estar, hay, gustar-type verbs (me gusta, me encanta, me duele), present tense (regular, stem-changing, irregular: tener, ir, hacer, poder, querer), reflexive verbs for routines, ir a + infinitive, preterite vs imperfect for the past, comparatives and superlatives, possessives, direct and indirect object pronouns, por vs para, telling time and dates, question words.
- Language B adds: present and imperfect subjunctive (after wishes, doubt, emotion, impersonal expressions, cuando + future meaning, para que, aunque), conditional and si clauses (si tuviera…, habría…), perfect tenses (he hecho, había hecho), the passive with se and ser + participle, imperatives (tú, usted, negative), reported speech, relative pronouns (que, quien, lo que, cuyo), connectors (sin embargo, por lo tanto, además, no obstante, a pesar de que), idioms and register.

## How to quiz it
- First check whether they take Spanish B or ab initio (their subjects in Settings say); pitch everything to that level.
- Vocabulary by theme or prescribed topic: English or a Spanish definition, ask for the word (with its article); or the word, ask for a sentence.
- Grammar: correct a sentence, conjugate in context, or translate a short sentence with a target structure (subjunctive after "quiero que", preterite vs imperfect in a story).
- Paper 1 drills: give a prompt in Spanish in IB style ("Escribe un correo electrónico a tu amigo sobre…"), ask for the opening, a paragraph or the full text, and mark on the criteria.
- Paper 2 style: a short text in Spanish with comprehension questions of the real kinds (verdadero/falso con justificación, respuestas cortas, emparejar).
- Oral practice: describe a visual stimulus in Spanish, then follow-up questions; for ab initio keep it to the prescribed topics.
- Feedback: corrections shown as original → corrección, the rule in one line in English, accents counted (está vs esta, sí vs si).
