# 04 · The notch, the answer card and on-screen overlays

Read `00-common.md` first.

## Scope
Everything Joe draws over other apps: the resting notch band, the recording pill, the answer card, what Joe is doing in the notch band (the task shelf), the hover overview, toasts, the greeting, live words, the screen pen, the screen pointer.

## Files
- `Notch.swift`: `Notch` (geometry, `screenUnderMouse`), `NotchPanel`, `NotchSpace` (pins panels to every Space via a private CGS API), `RestingNotch` (the black band with the sparkle, `ignoresMouseEvents`), `NotchGreeting`.
- `JoeMark` (Notch.swift): the logo at the notch, the app icon's orange four-point star drawn as a shape; used instead of the system sparkle on the resting band, pill, answer band and greeting. The band's ears are 56 pt (`RecordingPanel.ear`).
- `NotchOverview.swift`: hover overview (NotchNook-style). Polls the pointer every 0.04 s; opens after 0.08 s on the notch while Joe is idle (Raph wants it near instant: keep springs around 0.2-0.3 s response and content delays under 0.05 s); widgets clock, next class (tomorrow's first in the evening; with that subject's next deadline, matched on the class's first word), due soon (tap one: Joe helps start it), today's five (tap to tick off in the Daybook), Joe's tasks, next alarm, now playing (with the album cover from `Spotify.artwork()`, asked off the main thread each time it opens and after a skip), Ask Joe (three one-tap questions; asking hides the overview so the card can drop), focus timer (25 or 50 min, a real `.timer` alarm, counts down) (each has a `symbol` for the editor); order/selection saved in `notchWidgets`, edited with the drag editor in Settings. Data from `DashboardModel`.
- `AnswerPanel.swift`: the card (status steps, plan checklist, approval cards, reply). Once a reply is done: Copy, Save to notes (`NoteSaver.saveAnswer`, into the vault's Notes), and follow-up chips Simpler, Example, Quiz me that carry on the same conversation (`AppDelegate.followUpTyped`; Quiz me opens the study whiteboard through `openStudy`, on the card's subject). `MarkdownText` renders headings, lists, numbered lists, checklists, quotes, code, tables, rules.
- `RecordingPanel.swift` (pill), `LiveCaption.swift`, `TaskShelf.swift` (what Joe is doing, in the notch band itself since Oct 2026: background tasks show either side of the notch (alarms don't: Raph didn't want them up there; the Clock tile in the hover overview shows the next one), `TaskShelf.ear(for:)` sets how wide; click the band and the list drops out of the notch; no more capsule beside it or dragging; while the answer card is at the notch the band steps aside (`AnswerPanel.onShowingChange` → `refreshShelf`), since both hang from the notch and the band's count landed on the card's buttons; the open card's band says "N more working" instead), `Toast.swift`, `ScreenPen.swift` (draw over the screen; "Ask Joe about this" sends a screenshot), `ScreenPointer.swift` (point tags on screen from Claude's replies).

## Decisions
- One look and one motion for everything hung from the notch (resting band, listening pill, answer island, greeting, overview): `NotchShape` (rounded bottom, plus small concave shoulders where it meets the screen's top edge, like the iPhone) and the `NotchShape.opening` / `.closing` / `.growing` springs. Contents fade, scale and unblur in just after the shape opens and go first when it closes. The overview grows out of the notch (`OverviewModel.isOpen`) and shrinks back before its panel goes. Closed, every band shrinks to `NotchShape.tuck` above the notch's bottom edge, so it finishes behind the camera; the answer island's height closes in the same pass as its width (Raph saw the middle trail the edges). None of these windows has a system shadow (`hasShadow = false`): on a borderless panel it draws a light rim that shows as a white outline round the black shape.
- The resting band carries the study plan (Oct 2026): a study block on now (minutes left) or within the hour (its start time) in the right ear, and a red dot on the ✦ when Today's five has something overdue (`RestingNotch.updateStudy`, every 30 s from `StudyToday`). The overview's study widgets: Next study block (Start), Overdue (Upload/Email/Done), Projects (progress); the student default order is `NotchOverview.studentDefault`, and the overview widens for up to 7 tiles.
- Panels are recreated each time (a reused window could stay hidden behind full-screen apps) and pinned with `NotchSpace`.
- Two monitors: the alarm card measures its content (`fittingSize` before `sizingOptions = []`), or it grew onto the monitor above.
- The overview never opens while recording, with the answer card up, an alarm ringing, or the pen open.
