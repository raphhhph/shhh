# 07 · Study (IB DP and MYP)

Read `00-common.md` first.

## Scope
Quizzing, marking and revision: syllabus knowledge, the whiteboard, the quiz log, the Study page, the points calculator, MYP.

## Files
- `Resources/Syllabus/*.md`: our own subtopic-level maps for **Mathematics AI, Mathematics AA, Economics, Business Management, Environmental Systems and Societies, Geography, Global Politics, English A Language and Literature, English B, Chinese B, Spanish (B and ab initio), Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, History, Psychology, Theory of Knowledge**, plus `Command terms.md` (depth and IB marking) and `MYP.md` (criteria A–D per subject group with what each strand asks for, bands, 1–7 boundaries, Personal Project, eAssessment). Copied into the app by `build.sh`. Written in our words (IB guides are copyrighted). Unverified: ESS HL weights, Psychology's, Computer Science's and Global Politics' assessment details for their new guides (marked in each file), History's guide version.
- `StudyBuddy.swift`: pack lookup (`pack(for:)` aliases, matched at the start of a word so "business" isn't ESS), quiz log (`logURL`, `results()`), notch-quiz rules in the prompt (due topics first), hidden `<mark>` tag recording.
- `StudyReview.swift`: **spaced repetition** worked out from the quiz log each time (nothing extra stored). Topics are grouped by their number ("2.8", "AHL 1.12", "S1.4", "WH10") or name; one score per day; the gap grows 3 days → ×2.5 when ≥80%, ×1.3 when 50–79%, back to 1 day below 50% (caps 60/30 days). `due(subject:)`, `upcoming()`, `lines()` for prompts. Tests: calendar items in the next 14 days whose title says test/exam/quiz/assessment/summative/mock/paper n are matched to a subject (by its name, then its map's aliases); that subject's topics count as due if they'd come due before the test, and the soonest test sorts first ("before Econ Unit 3 test, Thu 23 Oct"). Cached 10 minutes. `coverage(subject:)`: the map's numbered subtopics against what they've been marked on; the tutor mixes in never-practised ones when nothing is due, and subject cards show a "Syllabus practised n of m" bar with "Something new". Used by the tutor prompt, the notch quiz rules, the Daybook build (one "Revise …" task) and the Study page.
- `PastPapers.swift`: the **past-paper importer**. Finds PDFs under `_files/School/` in a folder named for the subject (or `~/Library/Application Support/Shhh/Past papers/<subject>/` without a notes vault) whose path looks like a paper; reads each once (PDFKit text, Vision OCR for scanned pages), works out paper number, session, year, level and TZ from the name and cover, splits questions by "1." / "Question 1" lines in order, sums marks ("[Maximum mark: n]" or "[n]" per part), tags each question with the syllabus subtopic sharing the most rare words, and pairs papers with markschemes by those fields (same question number → markscheme pages). Index cached in `Past papers index.json`; a PDF is re-read only when it changes. `promptIndex` gives the tutor a file list plus one line per question, due and weak topics first. Study page "Add…" copies PDFs into `_files/School/<subject>/Past papers/`.
- `Mistakes.swift`: the **mistake book**. Every whiteboard answer that lost marks is appended to `School/Mistakes/<subject>.md` in their notes (or Joe's folder): date, topic, score, the question, the "✗ missing" points and the model answer. The tutor gets the latest eight and re-tests those points with fresh questions ("redo my mistakes" = only those). Study page due rows under 50% also have "Teach me". Subject cards show "Mistake book (n)" (opens it) and "Redo my mistakes".
- `Flashcards.swift`: **flashcards** for definitions. After each whiteboard quiz the tutor ends with a `<cards>` tag (one `term :: definition` per line: every define question plus key terms they missed, at most 12), shown as a card and saved to `School/Flashcards/<subject>.md` as `term::definition` lines (the Obsidian Spaced Repetition format). "Review my econ flashcards" (or "Review" in the Flashcards card on the Study page, which lists every subject's deck with "Make a deck" for empty ones) opens the deck; hold right ⌥ and say the definition and the card turns over showing what you said, with the term and definition read aloud when read-aloud is on on the whiteboard: shuffled, the ones pressed "Again" last time first; space turns over, 1 = Again (back to the end), 2 = Got it; the done screen offers "Quiz me on the hard ones". "Make flashcards on 2.8" has the tutor write a deck straight away.
- Routing (`AppDelegate.openStudy`): any request that says "whiteboard", "quiz me"/"test me" anywhere, "let's do a quiz", "give me a quiz", or "flashcards" opens the whiteboard. "Quiz me on that" (and a bare "Quiz me" from an answer card) adds the card's question as the topic. A bare "open the whiteboard" while a quiz is running brings that quiz back instead of starting a new one.
- Exam mode has a clock in the header: time on the question against the `time="<minutes>"` the tutor puts on the question tag (what the real paper allows); it turns orange when over. The summary card has Another quiz / Teach me my weakest topic / Exam question buttons. An answer said with right ⌥ while Joe is still marking goes into the answer box instead of being dropped.
- `BoardMath.swift` + `Resources/KaTeX/` (KaTeX 0.16.11, MIT, woff2 fonts only, plus `board.html`): **maths on the whiteboard**. `BoardText` shows a card's text as SwiftUI Text, or, when it has LaTeX (`\( \)`, `\[ \]`, `$$`, or a tight `$…$` not followed by a digit, so "$5" stays money), in a small WKWebView that typesets it and reports its height. The web view passes the scroll wheel on to the board. `BoardMath.speakable` turns LaTeX into words for read-aloud ("x squared", "dy over dx").
- `StudyBoard.swift`: the **whiteboard** window. `StudyBoardModel` (tutor session, tags `<question n marks topic source>`, `<ms>` hidden markscheme, `<feedback score total>`, `<model>`, `<summary>`, `<lesson title>` a short lesson card before the questions: on "teach me"/"explain", or before a topic scored under 40% twice), `StudyBoardView` (cards, composer, hint/skip/end, read aloud), `SketchPad` (drawn diagrams sent as JPEG; Claude marks the drawing). `tutorPrompt` builds the session prompt (pack, command terms, due topics, recent results, the past-paper index or the PDFs found, LaTeX for maths). `subject(in:)` also matches their own subject name.
- `Dashboard.swift`: `StudyPage` (Due for review card, Real past papers card with per-subject counts, "Past-paper question" and "Add…", subject cards with score, weakest topic and "Review n due", recent answers), `PointsCalculator` (/45 with the TOK×EE core matrix and the IB's failing conditions, including the HL/SL minimums when subject names say HL or SL).
- `AppDelegate.openStudy`: "quiz me…", "let's start/do a quiz", "study session", "open/pull up the whiteboard" open it; subject from the request, the card's conversation, or the last studied (`lastStudySubject`).
- Test commands: `--review` (the schedule), `--past-papers "<subject>"` (reads and prints the index), `--study`, `--route "<request>"` (says `whiteboard (quiz|exam|cards)` for study requests), `--board-preview <png> [sketch] [deck]` (`deck` shows the flashcard review; WKWebView maths doesn't show in off-screen snapshots).

## Decisions
- Real IB past papers are copyrighted and can't ship; Joe uses papers the student saves in `_files/School/<subject>/Past papers/` (with markschemes) and otherwise writes questions in each paper's style. ibo.org blocks automated access.
- Tutor session runs Haiku: first question ~6 s, marking ~5 s; accuracy via the hidden markscheme.
- MYP students (programme contains "MYP") are marked on criteria A–D out of 8.
- Voice answers go to the whiteboard only when it's the key window.
- KaTeX ships inside the app (600 KB) rather than being installed with npm like `mathdoc.js` uses: students won't have Node, and the whiteboard must work offline.
- The review schedule is derived from the quiz log, not stored, so hand edits to the log and old entries just work.
- Added past papers are copied (never moved) into their notes, so Obsidian shows them and other tools find them.
- The whiteboard tutor gets the past-paper index in its prompt (file list plus a line per question) instead of searching PDFs, so a real question comes up as fast as an invented one.

## Known issues / to-do
- New packs need a check against each subject's real guide by someone taking it (topic numbering follows the guides as we know them).
- Past-paper splitting is heuristic: papers whose questions aren't numbered "1.", "2." at the start of a line (some language papers, Paper 1 source booklets) index as one "whole paper" entry. Not yet tried on real papers (none saved on Raph's Mac as of 22 Oct); check with `--past-papers`.
- Spaced repetition only knows topics they've been marked on; topics never quizzed aren't scheduled (the tutor still picks those from the map).
- Maths cards are web views: they don't appear in `--board-preview` snapshots, and each costs a little memory (fine for a session's dozen cards).
- The notch answer card (AnswerPanel) still shows maths as plain text; it could reuse `BoardText`.
- MYP students get the MYP framework only, not subject content maps.
