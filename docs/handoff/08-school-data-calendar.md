# 08 · School data: Daybook, timetable, calendar, ManageBac

Read `00-common.md` first.

## Scope
Where Joe's knowledge of the student's day comes from and how it's shown: the Daybook, the timetable, calendar sources, deadlines, the morning briefing and evening check.

## Files
- `Daybook.swift`: `Daybook` (vault `Brain/Daybook.md`: focus, today's five, classes; `timeline(date)` from `_files/School/timetable.json`; `instructions` for the prompt; `recentRequests`; routines file path). **Local build**: `localText()` / `buildLocally()` write today's Daybook without Claude; `finishRules` tells Claude to fill only the parts marked `pendingMark`; `classNames` lists the timetable's classes.
- `CalendarFeed.swift`: `items(from:days:)` merges timetable lessons, alarms, `calendar.json` (Claude sync), **calendar links** (`links`, `refreshLinks()`, `parseICS`, `occurrences(of:rule:from:to:)`, `linkItems()`, ManageBac → deadlines), and the Mac's Calendar (EventKit). Duplicate deadlines from two sources are merged (`sameThing`, same day + ≥60% shared words). `Item.subject` is the class a deadline is for (`subject(of:notes:category:)`).
- `AppDelegate`: `greetIfMorning` (morning briefing on first wake, or after the wake-up challenge), evening check, night shutdown, calendar links timer (30 min).
- Dashboard: `CalendarPage`, `TodayTimeline`, `NowNext`, `DueSoon`.

## Decisions
- ManageBac and Google come in by their **iCal links** (pasted once in onboarding or Settings → Calendar links); Joe reads them himself. The Claude-based sync (`syncInstructions`) remains as a fallback when there are no links.
- Raph's ManageBac link is added (17 items read in testing).

- **The Daybook is built locally** (Oct 2026, branch `claude/school-data`). On the morning briefing or the first request of a day, Joe writes Classes (table from the timetable), Rest of today (link and Mac Calendar events), Deadlines (next three weeks), Today's five (what's due today or tomorrow, then the last day's unticked tasks with "carried N×" raised; 3× or more asks for a decision), Backlog and Inbox carried over, and a Focus line. Claude then fills only Needs you (Gmail), acts on Inbox, adds the weekday's regular meetings from Patterns and Post today. With no calendar link and no Mac Calendar access, Rest of today and Deadlines are also left to Claude (with the old calendar.json sync). `buildRules` (the full Claude build) is only the fallback when the local build can't write.
- With calendar links, the morning build no longer asks Claude to sync calendar.json (it duplicated the links).
- Calendar links: a link that fails this time keeps its last items (each saved item has `source`); an HTTP error says the link may have been reset. Repeating events (RRULE: daily, weekly with BYDAY, monthly, yearly; INTERVAL, COUNT, UNTIL, EXDATE, moved occurrences) are expanded for 90 days, so weekly Google events show every week. TZID times are converted to UTC; cancelled events are left out. On Google links, titles with test, quiz, exam, due, summative or formative count as deadlines (that check was dead code before).
- Deadline subject (`CalendarFeed.subject(of:notes:category:at:)`): Raph's real ManageBac feed (checked Oct 21) has only SUMMARY, DTSTART (`TZID=Asia/Shanghai`), DTEND, UID and a cut-off DESCRIPTION, with no class field, and many titles don't name the class ("Taxes", "Quiz: Markets 2"). So it tries, in order: a class named in the title (full name, initials such as BM or TOK, or a distinctive first word); then a class named in the notes; then typical subject words (`typicalWords`: taxes and markets for Economics, exhibition for TOK, and so on); Chinese characters for the Chinese class; the class on the timetable at the due time; and finally CATEGORIES. Due soon and the calendar card show the result.

- **Timetable from a photo or PDF** (for new students with no timetable.json): `Daybook.importTimetable(from:dayToday:) async -> String`. Claude (Sonnet, Read tool only) returns periods and days as JSON; `timetableJSON` turns that into timetable.json with dates for the next 183 days. A weekly timetable maps weekdays; a rotation (Day 1–N) moves on one day per weekday from `dayToday`, or Day 1 today. An earlier timetable goes to the Trash. Onboarding calls it from the School step. Weekly and cycle-less timetables have no "Day N · Cycle X" label.

- **Holidays**: `CalendarFeed.holidays` holds the days covered by whole-day events on the links or calendar.json named a holiday, break, vacation, half term, recess, National Day and the like. `Daybook.schoolDay(on:in:)` treats them as no school, so classes, the Daybook and "tomorrow" skip them for any timetable.

- **New ManageBac tasks during the day**: after each link refresh (every 30 min), deadlines whose title wasn't there before go into today's Daybook (`Daybook.addNewDeadlines`): under Deadlines, and as a task marked "new" in Today's five when due within two days and there's room, else Backlog.

- **Plan** section in the local Daybook (`studyPlan`): today's free periods (a "—" in the grid), 90 minutes starting 30 minutes after the last class, and 19:30–21:00 (10:00–12:00 instead on a day off), each given to the most pressing deadline of the next two weeks. The soonest gets two blocks; tests and summatives come first on the same day. Blocks work around timed events (they move past them, or are cut short), and any block left under 30 minutes is dropped. These are suggestions, not calendar events, so the timeline doesn't show them.

- **Day boundary at 04:00**: before 04:00 the Daybook still belongs to yesterday (`isCurrent` accepts that day, `archiveIfOld` and `buildLocally` wait). On Oct 22 Raph was up past midnight and Joe built Thursday's Daybook at 00:35, archiving Wednesday's while he was still working on it. A carried task loses its "· new" mark.
- Tested on nightly wave 2 (Oct 22, 00:52): `--daybook-preview` had every section; subjects were right for BM, Chinese B and English A L&L; the link refresh read 17 items; no errors in the log.

- **Loop-closer** (Oct 2026, branch `claude/study-buddy-followthrough`), all in `localText`: (1) **overdue sweep**: deadlines from the last 14 days that aren't done become one task each, `— overdue since Wed 24 Sep · upload it ([Open ManageBac](link)) / email the teacher / mark done` (the link is the feed item's `URL`/a link in its notes, else the ManageBac site from the calendar link, `CalendarFeed.manageBacHome`); three go in Today's five, the rest in Backlog. "Done" is `Daybook/Done.md` plus the last day's ticked lines; `buildLocally` copies ticked lines into Done.md (`recordDone`) so a handed-in task isn't raised again. Matching uses `sameTask` (≥80% of words, numbers count), stricter than `CalendarFeed.sameThing`. (2) **Ageing**: Backlog lines get `· added <date>` the first time they're seen; at 7 days up to two a day are raised into Today's five with "— decide: do, schedule or drop"; at 21 days they move to `## Dropped` at the bottom (not deleted). (3) **Never empty**: after urgent, overdue, project milestones, carried and raised tasks, Today's five is topped up from Backlog to at least three (project lines first, then oldest). (4) Today's event clashes go in Needs you (kept by Claude: `finishRules` says keep the lines there). `loopRules` (in `instructions`) tells Claude: email the teacher as a draft only, never send; "mark it done" ticks it everywhere and adds a Done.md line; how to handle "decide:" lines.
- `Daybook.rootOverride` lets the CLI point the Daybook (and CalendarFeed's calendar.json, projects, Week.md) at another vault.
- **Week planner** (`WeekPlan.swift`): `Daybook/Week.md` for seven days, built by `--week-plan` ("plan my week", an assistant rule) or on Sunday from 17:00 for the week ahead (`buildIfSundayEvening`, on the 30-minute calendar-links timer). Each day uses `Daybook.freeBlocks` (the Plan's free periods / after school / evening logic, now shared, with a 23:00 hard edge) minus timed events, and gives blocks to deadlines and project milestones by due date (tests, IAs, essays and milestones want 3 blocks, others 1). Day headings carry the ISO date. **Re-plan**: at the morning build (`buildLocally` → `WeekPlan.replan`), an unticked block on an earlier day is struck through and moved to the next free slot before its deadline; `## Moved` lists what moved; one that fits nowhere becomes a Needs you question. A block whose task is in Done.md is ticked instead. **Clashes**: overlapping events → one "X and Y both Thu 30 Oct 15:30: which one?" in Needs you, skipped once Patterns has a line naming both (Claude writes "- Clash: X vs Y → Y" there).
- `CalendarFeed.Item.url`: parsed from the ICS `URL` property, or the first link in DESCRIPTION.
- **Study loop in the routines** (`StudyLoop.swift`, branch `claude/study-buddy-routines`). Morning brief: `briefFacts` adds, worked out locally from the fresh Daybook and the projects, one overdue item with its action, today's Plan blocks and this week's project milestone, each said in one sentence. Night shutdown: `shutdownSteps` asks what got done (lists what's still open), ticks each with `--mark-done`, runs `--week-replan` (`WeekPlan.replan(tonight: true)`: today's blocks that are over and unticked move too), says tomorrow's first block (Week.md, else a plan worked out now) and offers one loop-close ("Taxes is still overdue: shall I draft the email to the teacher?", draft only). Sunday: `buildIfSundayEvening` now returns Week.md when it wrote it, and Joe shows "Next week planned" on the card (block count, first block, the clash questions; spoken when the voice is on), or a toast when he's busy.
- **Local study intents** (`StudyIntent`, no Claude): "start my EE / Econ IA due 15 Feb / TOK essay" → `IBProjects.create` (kind, subject, optional date; an IA without a subject, or extra words like "start writing my TOK essay intro", go to Claude); "plan my week" / "plan next week" → `WeekPlan.build` and a summary card; "mark X done" / "done with X" / "tick off X" → `StudyLoop.markDone` ticks it in the Daybook, Week.md and the project milestone and adds it to Done.md (only when exactly one open thing matches; else Claude); "how's my EE going" / "how many words is my EE" → the project's progress line. One line on the card, read aloud when the voice is on. Not for follow-ups on an open card (those stay with Claude, so the night shutdown's answers go through its steps). `loopRules` now tells Claude to use `--mark-done`.

## Test
- `Joe --daybook-preview [<vault>] [yyyy-mm-dd]` prints the Daybook Joe would build now, or for that day (07:00), from that vault (writes nothing).
- `Joe --week-preview [<vault>]` prints the week plan from today and what a re-plan of the saved Week.md would move (writes nothing). `Joe --week-plan` writes Week.md. `Joe --daybook` prints the instructions (and builds today's if missing).
- `Joe --prompt brief|shutdown|sunday [<vault>]` prints the study lines added to the morning brief, the shutdown's study steps, or the Sunday card for next week (writes nothing).
- `Joe --route "start my Econ IA" "plan my week" "done with Paper 2" "how's my EE going"` → `local (project new)`, `local (week plan)`, `local (mark done)`, `local (project status)`. `Joe --local "<request>"` does it and prints the card (it writes). `Joe --mark-done "<title>"`, `Joe --week-replan` (the shutdown's re-plan).
- `Joe --calendar-link parse <file.ics>` prints parsed items, repeats expanded.
- `Joe --timetable-import <photo or pdf> ["Day 3"]` reads a timetable and saves timetable.json (the old one goes to the Trash).
